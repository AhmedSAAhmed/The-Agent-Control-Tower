How the tower catches a rogue agent

  1. Input scan. Every inbound ticket is scored by Llama Prompt Guard 2 (pattern heuristic as fallback). Above 0.8 the task is tainted: any write action now needs a human.
  2. Per-call policy. The tool gateway checks scope (least privilege), egress allowlist, bulk-PII export, self-privilege changes, budget and business limits. A security denial is a strike.
  3. Behaviour. The guardian quarantines on 2 security strikes in 60 s, 3 denials in 60 s, or more than 12 tool calls in 30 s (runaway loop). It also tracks decision drift against the policy baseline.
  4. Containment. Credentials are revoked at the gateway, so even a model that keeps "trying" cannot reach a system. Nothing is lost: the frozen task and its evidence stay in the replay and the hash-chained audit log.
